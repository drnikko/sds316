Initially, I want to explore the dataset containing Washington state fiscal information. To simplify this exploration, I am looking at just the fiscal year 2025.

#python cell = CMD Shift I

import pandas as pd

#the data included a space behind each year
df["FiscalYear"] = df["FiscalYear"].str.strip()

df_2025 = df[df["FiscalYear"] == "FY 2025"]

How should I rigorously define “police spending” and “social spending”?

After just visually looking through some of the data, I’m seeing that a lot of spending items in this data are for municipal governments to support local law enforcement, courts, and crime prevention. This is making me consider that “police spending” may be a narrow term for this project, though I don’t think I should expand this term to “criminal justice spending”. A better term for this type of spending is something I am thinking about moving forward.

I used King County to obtain the spending items used in the mapping process because it is the largest county per capita and includes the city of Seattle. I figured it would likely have the most spending items, so when I applied the mapping to other counties, it would (hopefully) include all possible items.

For example, one spending item is judges’ salaries, this shouldn’t be included in police spending. This goes back to my earlier note about how I need to define “police/law enforcement” spending.
