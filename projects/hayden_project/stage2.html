About

These first four questions look at school demographics and incident/disciplinary reports.

Dataset

The dataset is NC DPI School Report Card Resources for Researchers. It is stored in the datasets_files folder. Since the file was so large I only included the files that I was planning to use, however the entire zipfile can be downloaded at the link above. In the datasets_files folder I also included the data dictionary that’s labeled SRC_Data_Dictionary_202425.xlsx

Research Question

I initially posed three research questions all of which are pretty broad and go in slightly different directions. I’m hoping by the end of Stage 2 I’ll be able to narrow it down and have a better understanding of that data that’s infront of me. The questions included: 1. How the geographic patterns of school/neighborhood segregation in Mecklenburg County may shape patterns of juvenile and young adult criminal legal system involvement, how residential segregation shapes the geography of police encounters in Mecklenburg County, and how the geographic distribution of racial and socioeconomic school segregation in Mecklenburg County has changed over time. I defintely can’t answer all of these in my project but am confident that the data and information for at least one of them exists.
